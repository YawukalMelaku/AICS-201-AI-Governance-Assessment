# AICS-201: AI Ethics, Governance & Legal Compliance Assessment

## Project Overview

This repository contains the practical work for Assignment 01 of
AICS-201: AI Ethics, Governance & Legal Frameworks.

The assessment evaluates the proposed AI-powered customer identity
verification, fraud detection, and financial risk assessment platform
of the fictional organisation SecureFinance Africa.

## Objectives

- Identify and assess ethical risks associated with AI deployment.
- Apply the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
- Evaluate relevant legal and regulatory requirements.
- Measure potential algorithmic fairness issues using synthetic data.
- Examine AI decision explainability and human oversight.
- Develop practical AI governance recommendations.

## Repository Structure

- `report/` — Report outline and final assessment report.
- `risk_register/` — AI risk register and risk assessments.
- `data/` — Documentation and synthetic datasets.
- `analysis/` — Python scripts for fairness and explainability analysis.
- `results/` — Generated tables, metrics, and visualisations.
- `evidence/` — Analysis notes and supporting evidence.
- `references/` — Research sources and references.

## Data Protection

All experimental data must be synthetic or fictional.

Do not include real customer records, bank information, credentials,
or confidential organisational data.

## Reproducibility

Python setup instructions and analysis commands will be documented
as the implementation is developed.

## Frameworks

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
- EU Artificial Intelligence Act, where applicable
- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
- OECD AI Principles
